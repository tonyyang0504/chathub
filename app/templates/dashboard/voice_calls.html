Twilio Configuration
Note: This feature is in development. Configure your Twilio phone number's webhook URL to:
${window.location.origin}/api/voice/incoming
How It Works
1 Someone calls your Twilio phone number
2 ChatHub answers and says "Please speak after the beep"
3 Caller speaks → Twilio transcribes the speech
4 AI generates a response
5 Response is spoken back to the caller via TTS
Pricing: Twilio charges ~$0.013/min for voice + $0.01/min for transcription. A 1-minute call costs about $0.023.
